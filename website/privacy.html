Privacy

TaskMaster runs entirely on your computer. It reads the process list and system counters from your operating system and keeps them in memory only while the app is open.

What it collects

Nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no telemetry and no network code in the app.

What it does to your system

It only reads process and system statistics. The one action it takes is the one you ask for: ending a process you selected. Nothing is uploaded.

This website

This site is static pages with no cookies and no trackers. It stores your light or dark choice in your browser's local storage.
